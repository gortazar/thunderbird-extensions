OpenVidu Meet

Connect Thunderbird to your OpenVidu Meet server

Server

The address of OpenVidu Meet, including its base path (/meet by default), e.g. https://openvidu.example.com/meet.

Generate it in OpenVidu Meet → Embedded → Generate API Key. It is sent only to your server, in the X-API-KEY header. It grants full control over every room, so use it only on computers you trust.

Meetings

Used when you join a room from the sidebar.

0 keeps them until you delete them. Rooms created from the sidebar are always kept.

How to use it

In a message: click Meet link in the compose toolbar to create a room named after the subject and insert its guest link, or to insert the link of an existing room.

Joining: open the OpenVidu Meet space in the left toolbar and click a room to join it as host in your web browser. The ⧉ button copies its guest link.
